# Platform FAQs — ready to paste

These are the questions hospital, clinic and pharmacy admins see under **Help & Support → Frequently Asked Questions**. They are stored in the database, not in code. The Super Admin adds them under **Hospital → FAQs**, **Clinic → FAQs** and **Pharmacy → FAQs**. Paste each question and answer as written; the facility type is given for each block.

Keep FAQs about the account, the plan and support. How-to questions about a screen belong in the in-app help (the **?** button), which follows every release.

---

## All facility types (add under Hospital, Clinic and Pharmacy)

**Q: How do I add staff and give them a login?**
A: Open Staff and choose the role (Doctors, Receptionists, Pharmacists, OT In-charges); nurses are under Nursing → Nurses. Add the person with their email and a temporary password; they change it after their first login. How many staff logins you can have depends on your plan (Administration → Plan & Usage).

**Q: A staff member forgot their password.**
A: Passwords are reset by the facility admin — there is no self-service reset, so nobody can take over an account from the login page. Open Staff, find the person, and set a new temporary password from their row menu → Edit; they change it after signing in.

**Q: What does my plan include, and how do I upgrade?**
A: Administration → Plan & Usage shows your plan, its expiry and how much of each limit you use. To add modules or raise a limit, call 9022915865.

**Q: My plan has expired. Is my data safe?**
A: Yes. Nothing is deleted. For 7 days after expiry everything keeps working; after that you can still sign in and see every record, but new registrations, visits and admissions are paused until the plan is renewed.

**Q: How do I raise a support ticket?**
A: Help & Support → Raise a Support Ticket. Describe what you were doing and what you saw; a screenshot helps. You can follow the reply under Your Support Tickets.

**Q: Where do I find how to do a task on a screen?**
A: Click the **?** at the top right of any screen. It shows answers for that screen and your role, step-by-step guides, and short tours. Press Ctrl+K (⌘K on Mac) to search.

**Q: Can I see who changed a record?**
A: Yes. Reports → Audit Logs lists every important action (bills, admissions, discharges, medicines given, settings changes) with who did it and when.

## Hospital only

**Q: Which screens did the latest update change?**
A: IPD discharges are finished in one **Discharge checkout** pop-up (collect, return any advance, print summary + bill, free the bed). Reception sees **Ready to go** patients as soon as the doctor plans a discharge. ICU is one screen (Beds · Patients · Housekeeping · History). Doctors have **Round mode** for the ward round. Nurses start on **Due Now**, which lists every dose, vitals round and sample due. The patient chart's tabs are grouped into Summary, Orders, Charts and Forms.

**Q: How do I set up wards, ICU and nurses?**
A: Rooms → Wards & Beds for general wards, Rooms → ICU Wards for critical care. Nursing → Nurses adds nurses; set a ward's nurse in-charge on the ward card. Help → Guides → "Set up your hospital" walks through it in order.

## Clinic only

**Q: Can a single doctor run the clinic without a receptionist?**
A: Yes. Settings → Operations Settings → Reception mode: Solo lets the doctor register patients, create OPD visits and collect bills. Both lets the doctor and a receptionist share the desk.

## Pharmacy only

**Q: How do I print GST-compliant bills?**
A: Settings → Invoice details: enter your GSTIN and drug licence numbers. They are printed on every bill.
